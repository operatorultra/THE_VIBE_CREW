# The Vibe Crew Platform Plan
## Vision
Build a platform that helps vibe coders move from fragile code to launch-ready products by combining technical assessment, expert matching, and scoped execution.
## Core Promise
Upload your product context, get a clear technical state assessment, get matched with engineers who know your stack, and receive a realistic scope and timeline to launch.
## Primary Audiences
- Pre-launch teams that built quickly and now need stability before release.
- Early-traction apps that launched but are hitting complex technical issues they cannot prompt their way out of.
## Platform Workflow
### 1) Assess the technical state of the product
- Intake: repo access, architecture questionnaire, blockers, launch goals.
- Outputs: technical health score, risk map, debt priorities, effort estimate, launch-readiness verdict.
### 2) Match with experienced engineers
- Match with one or more engineers based on stack, architecture patterns, domain, urgency, and availability.
- Optional composition: lead engineer plus specialists (frontend, backend, infra, QA) when needed.
### 3) Create clear scope and timeline
- Produce a scoped issue plan with priorities, milestones, acceptance criteria, and delivery timeline.
- Keep cleanup work execution-focused and tied to launch readiness.
## Engineer Onboarding (LinkedIn-first)
- Engineers onboard by submitting their LinkedIn profile URL.
- The platform enriches the profile into a structured engineering profile (stack expertise, seniority signals, domains, recent role context).
- Engineers are then suggested to products that match their stack and problem profile.
- Human QA validates top matches before handoff in early versions of the platform.
## Offer Design
- Core offer: Ready-for-Launch Sprint (2 to 6 weeks).
- Secondary offer: Post-Launch Stabilization Sprint for products with traction.
## Boundaries
- We do not run the client’s business or provide business strategy advice.
- We do not provide open-ended ongoing maintenance.
- We do not build net-new features during cleanup sprints.
## MVP Surface
- Founder side: assessment dashboard, match proposals, scoped plan, milestone tracker.
- Engineer side: enriched profile, stack tags, availability, fit score, engagement history.
- Ops side: review queue for match quality and scope quality.
## Success Metrics
- Time to complete assessment.
- Time to first qualified engineer match.
- Scope acceptance rate.
- Launch-readiness conversion rate.
- Regression and defect reduction after sprint completion.
