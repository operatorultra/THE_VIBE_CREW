# The Vibe Crew Platform Vision
## Vision
Turn The Vibe Crew from a service into a platform where vibe coders can quickly move from fragile code to production-ready systems with structured expert support.
## Core Platform Outcomes
- Assess the technical state of a product.
- Match each product with one or more experienced engineers who know the stack.
- Produce a clear, scoped plan and realistic timeline for execution.
## Target Users
- Teams preparing to launch and needing technical stability.
- Teams with launched products and early traction that are now blocked by deeper engineering complexity.
## Product Flow
1. Intake and assessment of architecture, risk, and technical debt.
2. Engineer matching by stack fit, domain fit, and availability.
3. Scoped roadmap with prioritized issues, milestones, and timeline.
## Engineer Onboarding
- Engineers submit a LinkedIn profile URL.
- The platform enriches that profile into structured expertise signals.
- Engineers are suggested to products based on stack and problem-match relevance.
## Engagement Boundaries
- Not a business strategy service.
- Not open-ended maintenance.
- Not a net-new feature factory during cleanup engagements.
